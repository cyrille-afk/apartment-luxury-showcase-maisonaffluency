# src/lib rules
- Comparator edition labels reuse the curator-pick edition/house helpers and rehydrate live edition and house metadata after draft restore — keeps mixed-maker house attribution without storing prices or inventing edition copy.
- Comparator dimension parsing is isolated in comparatorDimensions and converts only recorded length units into labelled metric/imperial rows; supplier inch duplicates are omitted and unknown text stays unconfirmed — avoids fabricated measurements and changes to PDF/product-page formatting.
- Comparator difference classification normalizes whitespace/case and material-list order, ignores empty slots and excludes pricing; only populated differing specifications are highlighted — avoids false differences or new pricing calculations.
- Comparator details read recorded variant dimensions/materials and explicit maker credits; parent and child groupings share pieces without changing identity. Comparison PDFs load pictures before download and retain full text — avoids blank exports or invented specifications.
- Comparison PDFs reuse pdfCoverTheme, embedded editorial/body fonts and CSS paper tokens; section-height pagination keeps product columns together, dimensions share comparatorDimensions, and direct embedded public swatches include a full aligned appendix. Covers read only accessible project display fields and every page gets total-aware footers — preserves screen parity without private metadata or pricing-policy drift.

- Per-install manifests reuse public/manifest.json with a stable id/scope and a validated one-time QR launch parameter; responses are no-store, root head owns the single manifest link, and standalone startup scrubs/stages the code before children mount — enables fresh-container handoff without sharing iOS storage or caching credentials in a worker.

- Curator-pick slugs: the set_curator_pick_slug trigger replaces truncated/ambiguous slugs (1–2 chars, cut-off untitled-piec…) with title slugs; curator_pick_slug_audit lists remaining truncated/duplicate slugs — truncated slugs made product pages resolve to other designers' items. Map retired slugs in legacyProductSlugs.ts so old URLs redirect.

- Room shortlist covers pass allowlisted plain-text cover, closing-message and contact fields to the shared PDF generator; closing-page text wraps and continues across unnumbered pages, with only valid email and HTTP(S) links; device-local metadata stays scoped by account, room and saved shortlist ID, separate from public share snapshots — prevents unsafe links, clipping and cross-shortlist reuse.
- Quote & Proforma list filtering (status, inclusive From/To dates, quick ranges) lives in src/lib/quoteFilters.ts as pure functions with unit tests in quoteFilters.test.ts — the page only renders them, so the rules stay testable and can't drift between views. Dates compare the studio's local calendar day, matching what the quote card prints.
