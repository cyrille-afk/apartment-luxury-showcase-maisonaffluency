# Lightbox dimensions audit — 22 October 2026

## Coverage
- All 796 curator picks (111 makers with products) and 1,110 Trade records scanned: 8,177 entries, 1,245 unique strings. Profiles with no products have no dimension rows to audit.
- Attempted 249 original-sheet URLs; 172 readable PDFs, 77 failed/unreadable/invalid references. Source review covered 182 curator products with readable sheets and five Trade-only sheets. A readable PDF does not establish every size.
- Corrected 28 records from explicit supplier evidence; every changed record read back and matches its linked Trade record. Prices, finish values and unsupported sizes preserved.
- Shared conversion corrected for cm/mm/metres, attached rug dimensions, parenthetical measurements; codes, sizes, angles, weights and supplier COM/COL equivalents preserved. Trade lightboxes stack seat/arm heights.

## Outstanding source verification
- 614 curator products lack a readable linked sheet; individual source status is recorded in the CSV. Some were separately verified on manufacturer sites.
- All 27 Gilles & Boissier products lack original-sheet links: conversions are mathematically checked, source dimensions remain unverified.
- MicMac 472 source conflicts internally: French cage H52 cm versus English H40.47 in (102.8 cm); total H120 cm. Stored H55 remains flagged pending manufacturer clarification.
- Twin Upholstered shares a Twin Wood sheet; exact upholstery-model applicability remains unverified.
- US Table linked sheet contradicts unitless labels; confirm exact model before altering them.
- Unitless table/shelf/console/tray-height records remain unconverted rather than guessing cm or mm.
- Park Place counter, Scala 220, Itys triple, Angelo M/H and other sizes lack exact model coverage; Repose F links to finishes, not dimensions.
- Missing dimensions/variant mapping (Chair Hello, Sandy Cove, Visible, Lipari and several rugs) remain source omissions, not conversion errors.
- Conica uses the current manufacturer's 24×13×H33 cm; a designer announcement differs. Manufacturer: https://www.obscurae.fr/appliques-murales/project-two-ky966-n329z-nnx53-2grpr-gx3zc
- Driades verified 440×770×H1075 mm: https://sollen-design.com/en/product-2/dryads/ and https://sollen-design.com/wp-content/uploads/fiche-technique-barstool-06.pdf
- Ondine verified W90×D93×H70 cm: https://www.leo-sentou.com/collection-faubourg/ondine-armchair

Catalogue corrections are saved; frontend publishing and manual GitHub sync remain. This is not a claim that every original specification was accessible or verified.
